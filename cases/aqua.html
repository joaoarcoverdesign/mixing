The Problem

The compatibility gap

Market Reality

Dating apps are built on proximity and aesthetics — but compatibility is invisible in a swipe. Most platforms introduce strangers with no shared reference point, leading to high churn and emotional exhaustion.

The Opportunity

Astrology has become one of the most culturally significant self-knowledge frameworks for the 18–35 demographic. Aqua places it at the centre of the matching engine — making compatibility legible and meaningful.

Root Cause

Dating applications frequently introduce incompatible individuals, resulting in confusion and disappointment for users seeking meaningful connections. The superficial matching logic — photos, location, mutual swipe — fails to surface the relational dynamics that determine long-term compatibility.

Design Hypothesis: If we make compatibility visible and grounded in a shared cultural framework (astrology), users will experience fewer mismatches and develop stronger trust in the platform's suggestions — reducing churn and increasing engagement depth.

Design Framework

Why · What · How

Why

People want meaningful connections

Users invest time, emotional energy, and vulnerability in dating apps. When matches consistently disappoint, trust in the product collapses. Aqua addresses the root need: not more matches, but better ones.

What

A compatibility engine powered by astrology

Aqua uses Synastry — the astrological technique of comparing two birth charts to assess relational dynamics — as the core matching signal. This creates a compatibility score that feels personal and meaningful, not algorithmic.

How

Personalised insights + discovery tools

Users receive their own astrological profile for self-awareness, compatibility breakdowns with each potential match, and curated discovery tools to find like-minded individuals — all within an intuitive, visually rich interface.

Core Feature

The Synastry Engine

Compatibility made visible

Synastry is the practice of comparing two astrological birth charts to assess the relational dynamics between individuals. In Aqua, each potential match generates a compatibility breakdown across key dimensions — emotional resonance, intellectual connection, physical attraction, and long-term potential — giving users a richer basis for decision-making than appearance alone.

Design Principles

What makes Aqua different

01

Compatibility Before Connection

The Synastry score is visible before any conversation begins — giving users informed context for every potential connection, not a post-hoc rationalisation.

02

Self-Knowledge as Engagement

Aqua's cosmic profile feature drives retention through self-discovery. Users return to learn about themselves — not just to browse matches.

03

Accessible Astrology

Complex astrological concepts are translated into plain language — no prior knowledge required. The design democratises a rich cultural framework.

04

Visual Language of the Cosmos

Deep indigo palette, orbital motion design patterns, and celestial iconography create an immersive identity that sets Aqua apart from generic dating app aesthetics.
