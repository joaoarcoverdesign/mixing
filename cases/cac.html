Car Air Comfort — Climate Control Reimagined

A mobile IoT application that lets drivers pre-condition their car's interior temperature before they even step inside — solving one of the most universal frustrations of driving in hot climates.

The Problem

The hot car problem

The Pain Point

In hot countries, entering a parked car during summer means sitting in temperatures that can reach 60–70°C. Air conditioning systems take 5–10 minutes to cool the cabin to a comfortable level — minutes spent sweating and waiting.

The Solution Space

Modern vehicles increasingly support remote start and HVAC control via connectivity modules. The design opportunity: a simple, intuitive mobile interface that turns this technical capability into a genuinely useful experience.

Root Cause Analysis

The problem is not just physical discomfort — it's a ritual disruption. Getting into a hot car interrupts momentum, forces drivers to idle or endure heat, and in cases with children or elderly passengers, can present genuine health risks. The current solution (remote start keys) is hardware-dependent, single-function, and unavailable on most vehicles.

Design Brief: Create a mobile app that allows users to remotely activate and control their car's air conditioning system — reaching the ideal temperature before they arrive at the vehicle. The interface must feel effortless, require minimal interaction, and communicate temperature state clearly.

Design Process

From research to prototype

Phase 01

Discovery — Mind Map

A comprehensive mind map was built to explore all dimensions of the problem: user contexts, technical constraints, competitor solutions, and edge cases (elderly users, children, pets, extreme heat scenarios).

Key Areas Mapped

  • User contexts (commute, shopping, school pickup, travel)
  • Temperature sensitivity profiles (health conditions, age groups)
  • Connectivity methods (Bluetooth, cellular, OBD-II)
  • Safety considerations (engine-on, garage detection, time limits)

Phase 02

Persona Development

Monica Bezerra was built from interview synthesis — a high-agency professional whose relationship with time and comfort reflects the core user segment: urban, working adults in hot climates who depend on their car daily.

Phase 03

User Journey Mapping

A full journey map traced Monica's experience from leaving her office to reaching a cool car — identifying the exact moments of frustration, the decision points, and the emotional arc the app needed to address.

Journey Stages

  • Preparing to leave (packing up, gathering keys)
  • App trigger point (opening CAC on the way to parking)
  • Waiting vs. immediate departure decision
  • Arriving at a pre-cooled vehicle
  • Ongoing temperature management during the drive

Phase 04

Wireframes to Hi-Fi Prototype

Sketch frames established the interaction model. Mid-fidelity wireframes validated the flow. High-fidelity screens brought the CAC visual identity to life — a dark automotive aesthetic with a signature green temperature accent.

Core Features

Design decisions

01

One-Tap Activation

The primary action — start cooling — requires a single tap. No navigation, no sub-menus. The design prioritises speed over configuration at the core interaction level.

02

Real-Time Temperature Feedback

A live cabin temperature display reassures users that the system is working. Seeing the temperature drop removes anxiety and eliminates the need to repeatedly tap or check.

03

Target Temperature Presets

Users can save preferred temperatures for different contexts — "Morning commute", "School pickup", "After gym" — reducing decision fatigue on repeat journeys.

04

Smart Safety Limits

Automatic shutoff after 10 minutes if no motion is detected near the vehicle. Garage detection disables activation in enclosed spaces. Safety without friction.
