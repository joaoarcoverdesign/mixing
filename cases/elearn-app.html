Overview

The challenge

Context

Jalasoft and Jala University run intensive technology bootcamps for LATAM engineers and CS students. The existing tools were fragmented — students, teachers, and administrators operated in siloed, ad-hoc workflows with no unified digital experience.

Mission

Design a cohesive educational platform that addresses the distinct needs of three user profiles, from onboarding to progress tracking and bootcamp management.

What We Found

Through stakeholder interviews across all three user groups, clear pain points emerged. Students wanted a more immersive and structured experience — not just exercises, but real design processes. Teachers lacked tools for syllabus visibility and student communication. Administrators needed systems to detect students at risk of dropout before it happened.

Core Design Tension: The platform needed to serve radically different mental models — a student learning by doing, a teacher managing a cohort, and an administrator monitoring health metrics — within a single unified system.

User Research

Voices from the field

Structured interviews with students, teachers, and administrators revealed three distinct sets of needs that shaped the entire design strategy.

Students

"Better onboarding experience, more focus on UI, real projects that included the entire design process — not just fragments."

Teachers

"We need syllabus visibility, staff communication channels, and a way to track where each student actually is in their learning journey."

Administrators

"If we could see early warning signals — who might drop out, who's falling behind — we could intervene before it's too late."

Proto-Personas

Understanding the users

Three proto-personas built from interview synthesis — grounded in real behaviours, not assumptions.

Persona 01

The Bootcamp Student

Junior engineer transitioning into design. Motivated but overwhelmed by lack of structure and unclear expectations. Wants guided real-world projects, not abstract exercises.

Key Needs

  • Clear onboarding that sets expectations from day one
  • UI-focused lessons with tangible visual outcomes
  • Access to the full design process — research through handoff
  • Feedback loops that show progress

Persona 02

The Bootcamp Teacher

Senior designer or experienced practitioner tasked with structuring and delivering curriculum. Manages cohorts without dedicated communication tools.

Key Needs

  • Syllabus builder with visibility into delivery schedule
  • Direct communication channel with students and staff
  • Per-student progress tracking and performance signals
  • Resource library for sharing materials

Persona 03

The Bootcamp Manager

Operational administrator overseeing multiple bootcamp cohorts. Needs macro visibility to identify risk before it becomes attrition.

Key Needs

  • Dashboard with cohort health indicators
  • Early warning system for at-risk students
  • Bootcamp configuration and scheduling tools
  • Reporting for outcomes and completion rates

Design Process

From insight to interface

Step 01

Discovery

Stakeholder interviews across all three user groups. Affinity mapping to cluster pain points by theme.

Step 02

Synthesis

Empathy mapping and Value Proposition Canvas for each persona. "How Might We" exercises to reframe challenges as opportunities.

Step 03

Architecture

Information architecture defining role-based access, navigation structure, and content hierarchy for each user type.

Step 04

Wireframes

Low and mid-fidelity wireframes for priority screens. Iterative cycles with stakeholder feedback at each step.

Step 05

UI Design

High-fidelity interface using Manrope and Nunito typefaces, with a primary palette anchored by #EF293D — the Jalasoft brand red.

Step 06

Prototype

Interactive Figma prototype with full user flows for all three personas — validated in usability sessions with real bootcamp participants.

Outcomes

Design impact

01

Unified Multi-Role Platform

Three distinct user experiences — student, teacher, administrator — delivered within a single coherent design system. Each role sees only what is relevant to them.

02

Research-First Culture

The project introduced structured UX research practices within Jalasoft's design process — interviews, personas, and journey maps became team standards.

03

Dropout Prevention Signal

The admin monitoring dashboard provides early warning signals — a direct design response to the attrition problem identified during discovery.

04

Real-World Process Embedded

The student project submission flow mirrors professional design workflows — creating a pedagogical feedback loop between the platform's UX and what it teaches.
