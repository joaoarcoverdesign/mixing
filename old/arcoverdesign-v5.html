The embedded model

Senior design, every day

(04)

We work as a dedicated part of your team — embedded, async, and available daily.

You get senior UX/UI thinking applied consistently to your product, week after week, without the commitment of a full-time hire.

Step 01

Let's talk about your product

No sales call required. A short conversation about what you're building, where design is blocking you, and whether we're the right fit.

Step 02

We embed into your team

We join your Slack, set up in Figma, and align on priorities. We're inside your workflow from day one — no ramp-up lag.

Step 03

Async design, every day

Daily progress, delivered asynchronously. You review in Figma, we iterate fast. Every delivery includes a Loom walkthrough explaining our decisions.

Step 04

Strategic partnership

We challenge assumptions, surface UX risks, and keep the design quality bar high — not just executing briefs, but thinking ahead with you.
